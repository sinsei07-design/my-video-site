How to Use JP Vault Video

JP Vault Video is a simple search tool designed to help international users discover Japanese video and image content. This guide explains how to get the most out of the site.

1. Search by keyword

Type any word into the search box on the homepage and press "Search." Your keyword will be used to search external platforms and open the results in a new tab.

2. Use quick genre buttons

Below the search box, you'll find genre buttons like Ranking, New, Popular, Amateur, HD, and Recommended. Clicking any of these instantly searches that genre for you, without typing anything.

3. Check popular searches

The "Popular searches" section shows commonly searched terms, including cosplay, school uniform, kimono, maid, and shrine maiden themes. These reflect popular Japanese cultural and costume genres.

4. Understand what this site does

JP Vault Video does not host or store any video or image files. It simply provides a convenient search interface that links to external services. All actual content is hosted elsewhere.
