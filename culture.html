Popular Japanese Costume Genres

Japan has a rich tradition of costume culture, from historical clothing to modern subculture fashion. Here are some of the most searched genres on JP Vault Video, along with a brief introduction to each.

Cosplay

Short for "costume play," cosplay involves dressing up as characters from anime, games, and other media. It's one of the most globally recognized aspects of Japanese pop culture.

School uniform (Seifuku)

The Japanese school uniform, known as "seifuku," is a distinctive cultural symbol often featured in Japanese media and fashion.

Kimono

The kimono is a traditional Japanese garment with a history spanning centuries. It remains an iconic symbol of Japanese culture, worn during festivals and special occasions.

Maid

Maid fashion, inspired by Western maid uniforms, became a distinct part of Japanese subculture, especially associated with maid cafes in areas like Akihabara.

Shrine maiden (Miko)

The "miko" is a shrine maiden who assists in traditional Shinto ceremonies. Their distinctive red and white attire is a recognizable part of Japanese spiritual tradition.
